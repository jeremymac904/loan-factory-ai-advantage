# \[Marketing\] MARKETING POLICY

# **Marketing and Advertising Policy**

As a Loan Officer (LO) at Loan Factory, you must follow this Marketing and Advertising Policy. This ensures your materials are truthful, compliant, and meet all regulations. Loan Factory will review your marketing content and may request changes when needed. By working for us, you agree to these guidelines.

#### ***1\. Marketing and Advertising Costs***

What Loan Factory Provides (Free):

* Company-approved, compliant marketing materials & tools (Facebook Ads, Lead Funnel, etc) through our internal Marketing Features.

What You Pay For: All optional and personalized marketing expenses, including:

* Advertising campaigns  
* Print materials  
* Lead purchases  
* Services from third-party vendors

Any marketing services or materials you purchase outside the Company are voluntary and will not be reimbursed by Loan Factory. This policy ensures compliance with branding standards, regulatory requirements, and internal compliance guidelines.

#### ***2\. Approval before posting***

Before posting any mortgage-related content or advertising: 

* Submit your material for review here: [**click me**](http://loanfactory.com/my_escalation_desk?department=32866357041&_e=new)  
* Marketing will audit your social media monthly and send reminders via [To-do](https://www.loanfactory.com/my_todo) to ensure compliance.

* **Company Name and NMLS:** If you mention your **name**/ **website/ mortgage** in a post, you must also include: The **company name** & **NMLS,** and **your NMLS.**  
  **3.5.2 For Loan Officers licensed in Arizona (AZ) only:** 

  Include the company AZ-license (BK-2005457) and your AZ-license number in each post (either in image or text/ caption).

**3.6** **What NOT to Post**

* **Prohibited Content:** Loan Officers (LOs) are strictly prohibited from posting **false, offensive, discriminatory, or misleading information** on social media or any digital platform.  
* **Client Privacy:** LOs must **not disclose clients' non-public personal information (NPI)** in any form of digital marketing or advertising. Disclosure of such information is only allowed with **verbal or written consent** from the client (must have a record).  
  * Personal information includes, but is not limited to: Full name, Address, Social Security Number, Date of birth, Employment details, Financial information  
* **Trigger Terms Compliance:**  
  * If your post mentions: “Down payment,” “Monthly payment,” “Term,” “Finance charge,” etc., → You **must** include full loan details or a sample scenario.  
* **Rate Advertising:** Any post mentioning **interest rates**   
  * Must include the **APR** (Annual Percentage Rate). The **APR** must be displayed using the **same font style, size, and prominence** as the interest rate.  
* **Claims and Accuracy:** Do **not** make unsupported claims such as: “Lowest rates”, “Best rates”, “No closing costs”, “Guaranteed rates”, etc.

**3.11** **For Loan Officers with a Realtor License:**

Loan Officers who hold a Realtor license with any brokerage other than our California brokerage (MOSO) must not include any Realtor information \- including, but not limited to, titles, company names, license numbers, logos, or real estate branding \- in their Loan Factory email signatures, websites, or any marketing materials.  
Only California-licensed agents who are officially affiliated with MOSO may use a dual-role (MLO \+ CA Real Estate) email signature, and it must follow our approved format.

#### ***2\. Mandatory Marketing Review and Approval*** 

All self-created marketing materials related to the $2,000 Best Price Guarantee must be submitted to the Marketing Team (via [Escalation Ticket](https://www.loanfactory.com/my_escalation_desk?department=32866357041&_e=new)) for review and approval **before posting or publishing**.  
This applies to all platforms, including but not limited to:

* Social media posts  
* Flyers  
* Videos  
* Ads  
* Websites and landing pages  
* Email marketing

#### ***2\. State Restriction – Washington***

The $2,000 Best Price Guarantee must not be advertised, promoted, or marketed in Washington state. Loan Officers are responsible for ensuring their advertising targeting, audience selection, and distribution settings exclude Washington state. 

Failure to follow these requirements may result in the removal of the marketing material and additional compliance action if necessary.
